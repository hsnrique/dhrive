### Your OK, your rules
- Nothing is edited without your OK: each plan waits for you to approve it, edit it, or reply to change it. One switch lets the code start right after the plan.
- When Claude wants to run something it isn't allowed to, it asks: allow once, always in this project, or deny.
- Your own instructions, like an AGENTS.md for every project, plus rules for a single conversation.

### Stay in flow
- Conversations per project in the sidebar, with the sessions you had in the Claude app, the terminal or Codex beside them.
- `⌘K` finds any conversation by anything that was said in it.
- Mention files and folders with `@`, attach screenshots, and open any file in a side panel.
- Watch each edit as Claude makes it. Requests queue while one runs, and you get a notification when it needs you.
- See how full each session's context is, and compact it before it compacts itself.
- Hover Claude Code or Codex to see your plan's limits, live. Click it to see every MCP server, skill and plugin it has connected.

### Finish the job
- Every code step lists the files it changed, with the diff and an undo.
- Commit only that step's files, on a new branch, and open the pull request from the same place.

### Your account
- Sign in once with your email or Google. You get 14 free days, no card.
- Then US$15 a month or US$120 a year. Your account on dhrive.app shows your days, your plan and where to manage it. Your CLIs keep their own plans.
