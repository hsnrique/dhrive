title: The autonomous company
---
Say what company should exist, and dhrive starts it and runs it: the product, its website and its documents, task after task, from the CLIs you already use.

### The company
- One screen with everything side by side: tasks, website, documents and brief, with the chat beside them. The sidebar folds away.
- Tasks are suggested from the brief and worked one after another. Turn on **Ask first** to OK each one, or **Auto mode** to let it run for a few hours.
- The chat knows what the company did and turns what it suggests into tasks.
- Mission, roadmap, design guide and market research, written from what it built.
- Pick each step's model and effort.

### The website
- **Build** checks it on your Mac first, and **Fix it** sends a failed build to a task. Publish puts it at its own address on dhrive.site.
- Every version is kept, and any earlier one can go live again.
- Connect your own domain: dhrive shows the two DNS records to set and what's still wrong until it's live. Star the address the company goes by.
- A preview sits under the versions; it opens the full site, the live one or the one running on your Mac.

### Your data, kept
- Each company's tasks and timeline live in its own folder, so they travel with it.
- Put a company on GitHub in one click; after that, every finished task is backed up to its private repo.
- Deleting a company asks for its name, takes its site down and frees the name. Its folder goes to the Trash, and its conversations are put away rather than erased.
