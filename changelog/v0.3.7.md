title: Steadier, and in reach
---
- **Stop means stop**: nothing starts or lands after it. A file dhrive can't read is never written over, Auto mode no longer spins when its next tasks can't start, and a conversation and the company's tasks take turns in the same folder.
- **Needs your OK, everywhere**: every task an army runs can ask, and it shows on the company's page, in the sidebar and first in the list. What failed has its own group, with Try again.
- **Auto mode stops gently**: after the task it's on, or now. It warns before it starts when Ask first would make it wait.
- **Faster where you look all day**: a conversation no longer redraws on every log line, and the sidebar is there as soon as dhrive opens.
- **Keyboard and screen readers**: Control-Tab leaves a terminal, dialogs hold focus and give it back, approvals and finished tasks are announced, and ⌘= zooms.
- **Calmer screens**: one line per panel, one look for every button, errors that stack instead of replacing each other, and drafts kept in every composer.
