- **The app's code is open** at [github.com/hsnrique/dhrive](https://github.com/hsnrique/dhrive), under the Functional Source License: read it, change it, send a pull request.
- **Feedback** goes through your dhrive account, so the app carries no key of any service.
- **Already have dhrive?** Download it once more from [dhrive.app](https://dhrive.app): updates now come from the open repository, and they are automatic again from here.
