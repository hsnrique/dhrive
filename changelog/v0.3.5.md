title: Agent Army
---
- **Agent Army**: a company gets its own agents, each with a name, a role and its own CLI and model. Recruit the squad (Back, Front, QA, Docs, Growth) or hire your own.
- **Three at once**: up to three tasks run side by side, each in its own copy of the project. They land one at a time, only after the checks pass.
- **Tasks that wait**: a task can wait for the ones it needs, and each agent hears what the others delivered.
- **Folders an agent owns**: give an agent its folders, and its changes stay inside them.
- **Review before landing**, if you turn it on: the company's agent reads each change and sends back what isn't right.
- **Backup**: near its plan's limit, a task moves to the CLI you picked as its agent's backup, or waits for the plan to reset.
- **A new look**: flat panels, square corners and IBM Plex.
